I lead the OCIO (outsourced chief investment officer) asset management team at Mirae Asset Securities, which manages public funds, corporate mandates and other institutional assets. Before that I worked in derivatives structuring, principal investment and digital-asset business development.

My research has two strands. The first is using large language models and multi-agent systems to make research and portfolio work more productive. The second is measurement: checking, on real market data, the things that are easy to overstate, such as benchmark leakage, trading costs and the diversity of model proposals.

CallRank: Isolating What Changed in Earnings-Call Q&A for Sector Ranking and Cost-Adjusted Alpha

Measures how each firm's earnings-call Q&A changes relative to its earlier calls, ranks sectors on that change, and evaluates the strategy after trading costs.

Doohwi Cha, Minsuk Sung, Seunghan Son, Juyeong Lee, Donghwa Seo, Minjae Lee, Hyeonjun Yeo, Yoontae Hwang

ACM ICAIF 2026 Main TrackPoster Code →

Metropolitan Housing Signals for Treasury Duration Risk Management

Builds a walk-forward risk warning from metropolitan housing data, links it to a bounded Treasury-duration rule, and tests whether it reduces losses against a 3–5-year Treasury benchmark after costs.

Doohwi Cha, Hyeonjun Yeo, Gyuil Jung, Jeongkyoo You, Minsuk Sung, Donghwa Seo, Minjae Lee, Seunghan Son, Juyeong Lee, Yoontae Hwang

ACM ICAIF 2026 Main TrackProceedings Code →

The Division of Research: Information Access and Independent-Book Capacity in LLM Investment Teams

In a seven-model LLM investment team, giving every model the same information yields about two effective independent portfolios (Neff 2.05). Splitting information access raises this to 3.98 and reduces prompt tokens by 68%. The study measures the diversity of proposals, not returns.

Doohwi Cha, Minjae Lee, Minsuk Sung, Juyeong Lee, Donghwa Seo, Seunghan Son, Yoontae Hwang
